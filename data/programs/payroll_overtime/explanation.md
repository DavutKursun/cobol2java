The program calculates weekly pay from hours worked and an hourly rate. The first 40 hours are paid at the normal rate; hours above 40 are overtime, paid at 1.5 times the rate and rounded to 2 decimals. Tax is 20% of gross pay when gross is above 5000, otherwise 10%, rounded to 2 decimals. Regular pay, overtime pay, gross pay, tax and net pay are printed right-aligned in a 10-character edited field (PIC Z(6)9.99).
